How to get your CSV URL from Google Sheets

  1. Open your Google Sheet

  3. Choose the tab (Recovery or Outstanding), select CSV format
  4. Click Publish and copy the URL
  5. Paste it below and click Load

  3. Come back to this dashboard and it shows the latest data on every page load
